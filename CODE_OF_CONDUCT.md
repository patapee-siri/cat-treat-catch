# Code of Conduct

This is a small, friendly hobby project. Everyone is welcome, whatever their
background, experience level, language, or identity.

## Our standard

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep feedback about the work, not the person.

Examples of good behaviour:
- Being patient with newcomers and with language differences (Thai and English are both fine here)
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people playing it

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or comments
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing other people's private information without permission

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and contributions that
don't fit this code, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the
maintainer through GitHub ([@patapee-siri](https://github.com/patapee-siri)); reports are
handled privately.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
